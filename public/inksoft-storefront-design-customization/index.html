Make your InkSoft store easier for customers to browse and shop. FJX Studio supports storefront content and presentation updates for custom apparel businesses, including approved banners, category organization, product imagery and store information.

Discuss Your InkSoft Project

What this service includes

Support is scoped around your InkSoft account, product data, permissions and operating procedures. Your team supplies and approves business-critical pricing, artwork, inventory and production decisions.

Storefront content and visual updates

Add or update approved store banners, promotional messaging, brand details, page text and supporting images. We work within the theme controls and access available to your account.

Navigation and category organization

Arrange product categories, collections and menu labels so customers can find team apparel, uniforms, spirit wear, accessories and other merchandise with fewer unnecessary clicks.

Product presentation

Improve product listing consistency through approved product titles, images, descriptions, option labels and featured-product placement. Artwork and product details are supplied or approved by your team.

Mobile and pre-launch review

Review key storefront pages at common screen sizes, check navigation and prominent links, and document issues that require your decision or platform-level support.

A clear, reviewable workflow

1. Share your requirements

Tell us about your store, the products involved, the current process and the result you want to achieve.

2. Provide source data and access

Supply the approved files, account permissions, reference examples and decision rules needed for the assigned tasks.

3. Review a sample

We complete an agreed sample or first batch so your team can confirm field mapping, presentation and accuracy.

4. Complete and report

We carry out the approved work, record exceptions and hand back items that require your decision.

Frequently asked questions

Can you customize the look of my InkSoft store?

We can update supported storefront content and presentation elements using the permissions and theme options available in your InkSoft account. The exact scope depends on your store setup.

Can you add banners and category images?

Yes. We can place supplied or approved banners and images, update category content and help organize the storefront.

Do you provide custom coding or theme development?

The standard service focuses on supported storefront settings and content updates. Custom development is assessed separately and is not assumed to be available in every InkSoft account.

Can you update an existing store without rebuilding it?

Yes. Most requests can be scoped as targeted updates to the current storefront, product presentation or navigation.
