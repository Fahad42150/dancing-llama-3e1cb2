Frequently asked questions

Do you provide QuickBooks bookkeeping services?

FJX Studio provides administrative assistance such as data entry, document organization and record maintenance. We do not represent this service as CPA, tax preparation, audit or licensed accounting advice.

Can you enter invoices and expenses?

Yes. We can enter details from documents and instructions you provide, and flag missing or unclear information for your review.

Can you reconcile accounts or approve payments?

Financial judgments, account reconciliations, payment release and approvals should remain with your bookkeeper, accountant or other authorized personnel. We can help organize source documents and tracking information.

Do you work with QuickBooks Online?

We can discuss tasks in QuickBooks Online where suitable access and instructions are provided. Please confirm your edition and workflow during project scoping.

Do you support US and Canadian businesses?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote administrative support to businesses in the United States and Canada.

How do we get started?

Share your recurring bookkeeping admin tasks, QuickBooks setup, document workflow and required coverage so we can define a suitable scope.
