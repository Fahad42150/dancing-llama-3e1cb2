How the design process works

1. Share your brief

Send your goals, audience, preferred style, brand assets, copy and required sizes or placements.

2. Confirm scope

Agree the deliverables, number of concepts, revision rounds, file formats and timeline before production.

3. Review the designs

Review draft concepts and provide consolidated feedback within the agreed revision scope.

4. Receive final files

Receive approved exports in the agreed formats and dimensions, organized for your intended channels.

Frequently asked questions

Can you design a logo for a new business?

Yes. We can create logo concepts based on your business name, audience, preferred style and references. Trademark searches, legal clearance and registration are not included.

Do you design Instagram Reels and YouTube thumbnails?

Yes. We design cover images and thumbnails sized for the relevant platform. Video editing is a separate service and can be scoped independently.

Can you create regular social media graphics?

Yes. We can prepare recurring post, carousel, story and campaign designs using your content plan and brand guidelines.

Which file formats can you provide?

Final formats depend on the deliverable and agreed scope. Common exports include PNG, JPG and PDF; editable source files can be discussed at project kickoff.

Do you provide printing or social media posting?

This page covers design and digital asset preparation. Printing, publishing and account management are not included unless separately agreed.

Do you work with clients in the USA and Canada?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote design support for businesses serving customers in the United States and Canada.
