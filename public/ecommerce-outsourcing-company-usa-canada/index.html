Frequently asked questions

What does an eCommerce outsourcing company do?

An eCommerce outsourcing provider takes on defined online store workflows such as product catalog maintenance, order administration, inventory data entry, customer support administration and routine store updates.

Is FJX Studio a digital marketing agency?

FJX Studio's focus on this page is eCommerce operations outsourcing and virtual assistant support. This is not a claim to provide a full-service digital marketing agency offering such as paid media, SEO campaigns or web development.

Can a small business outsource eCommerce tasks?

Yes. A small business can outsource a clearly defined project or recurring task list without delegating account ownership or business approvals.

Which platforms can you support?

InkSoft is our primary specialty. Shopify and WooCommerce operations can be discussed, and Amazon, OrderMyGear and Printavo support are scoped to the specific administrative tasks requested.

How do you protect store access and information?

Access permissions, confidentiality expectations, data-handling instructions and approval responsibilities are agreed before work begins. We use the access level provided by the client and do not require master owner credentials for routine tasks.

Do you work with businesses in the USA and Canada?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote support to businesses serving the United States and Canada.
