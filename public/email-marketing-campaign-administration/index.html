Email Marketing Campaign Administration

What this service includes

Email marketing requires accurate lists, consistent formatting, links, testing and careful scheduling. We provide operational support for campaigns your team plans and approves, with attention to subscriber preferences and platform rules.

Newsletter formatting

Format supplied copy, images, buttons and links into a consistent email layout using your brand assets.

Campaign setup and scheduling

Enter approved subject lines, preview text, sender details and campaign content, then schedule after your authorization.

Mailing list organization

Import approved contacts, organize segments and tags, and flag obvious formatting issues or duplicates.

Template maintenance

Update reusable email templates, footer details, brand elements and standard content blocks.

Pre-send quality checks

Review links, images, personalization fields, rendering basics and required footer elements against your checklist.

Campaign record keeping

Maintain send calendars, campaign names, asset folders and performance exports for your team's review.

How FJX Studio supports your workflow

1. Confirm your email platform

Review platform access, subscriber sources, consent records and existing campaign templates.

2. Prepare campaign assets

Receive approved copy, creative assets, audience segments and send instructions.

3. Build and quality-check

Format the campaign, test links and content, and share a preview for approval.

4. Schedule and document

Schedule only after approval and record campaign details and available performance data.

Frequently asked questions

Do you write the marketing strategy?

This service focuses on campaign operations and formatting. Strategy, offers, claims and final copy are supplied or approved by your business.

Can you manage Mailchimp or Klaviyo?

We can assess the specific platform and administrative tasks. Support depends on access, training and platform permissions.

Will you send campaigns without approval?

No. Campaign content, audience and send timing require your approval before scheduling or sending.

Do you guarantee opens, clicks or sales?

No. Email performance depends on list quality, content, offer, timing and many other factors. We do not guarantee campaign results.
