Chipply online store setup and management assistance

Chipply is designed for team dealers, apparel decorators and promotional product distributors. Its store workflows include store setup, product selection, templates, artwork placement, inventory-aware product options and order processing. Frequently asked questions

Who uses Chipply?

Chipply describes its platform for team dealers, apparel decorators and promotional product distributors, including businesses operating team and branded merchandise stores.

Can you help set up a Chipply store?

We can review your store setup checklist and discuss administrative assistance within the permissions and workflows available to your Chipply account.

Can you manage Chipply products and artwork?

We can assist with assigned catalog and artwork coordination tasks using assets and specifications provided or approved by your team.

Can you handle Chipply order processing?

We can support agreed administrative order tasks and tracking. Purchasing, production, fulfillment and financial actions remain subject to your team's procedures and authorization.
