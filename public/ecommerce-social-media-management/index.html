Frequently asked questions

What does eCommerce social media management include?

It may include content calendars, product post preparation, scheduling, approved publishing, promotion coordination and basic activity reporting.

Can you create posts for product launches and seasonal promotions?

Yes. We can organize post drafts and schedules for launches, new products, seasonal collections and store promotions using your approved information.

Do you guarantee sales or followers?

No. Social media outcomes depend on many factors. FJX Studio provides agreed content and account administration support, not guaranteed growth or sales.

Can you reply to customer messages?

Customer responses can be included only with agreed response guidelines, permissions and escalation rules. Sensitive complaints, refunds and order decisions should be referred to your team.

FJX Studio provides remote support to businesses serving the United States and Canada. Scope, platform access, responsibilities and approval procedures are agreed before work begins.
