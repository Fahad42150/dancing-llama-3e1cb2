Frequently asked questions

Can you create an original logo for our team?

Yes. We can create original logo concepts based on your team name, sport, colors, audience and preferred style.

Can the design be used on uniforms and merchandise?

We can prepare agreed artwork exports and layouts for apparel and merchandise production. Your printer or uniform supplier should confirm its technical file requirements before production.

Can you design a mascot as well as a logo?

Yes. Mascot artwork can be developed as part of a logo package or scoped as a separate design deliverable.

Can you copy a professional team's logo?

No. We create original concepts and do not reproduce protected professional, collegiate or third-party team logos without documented authorization. We also do not imply official league or team affiliation.

What files will we receive?

Common exports include PNG, JPG and PDF. Vector or editable source files can be discussed and specified in the project scope.

Do you work with teams across the United States?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote design support for teams and organizations throughout the USA.
