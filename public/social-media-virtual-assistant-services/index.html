Frequently asked questions

Can your virtual assistant work with our existing software?

We can work with supported tools when access, instructions and required permissions are provided. Please identify your systems during the project discussion.

Can we start with part-time support?

Yes. The workload, coverage and schedule can be scoped around your recurring needs and agreed availability.

How do you handle approvals and sensitive tasks?

We follow your documented approval and access procedures. Financial decisions, legal or regulated advice, account ownership changes and other sensitive approvals remain with authorized client personnel.

Do you support businesses in the USA?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote business support for clients in the United States and Canada. Coverage hours are agreed at kickoff.

What happens if a request is outside the agreed scope?

We flag it for review and confirm scope, timing and any additional requirements before proceeding.

How do we get started?

Send a short description of your business, recurring tasks, software and preferred coverage. We can then discuss a suitable support arrangement.
