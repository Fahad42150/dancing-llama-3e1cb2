Product collections, employee access, location-specific assortments, budgets or allowances, approval steps, order cutoffs and fulfillment updates must work together. We help maintain the store operations and records around the platform your business already uses.

Frequently asked questions

Can you configure employee budgets or allowances?

We can help document and maintain the rules and data you provide, and assist with platform settings where your account supports them. Your finance and HR teams retain control of policy and approvals.

Can one company store support multiple locations?

We can help organize location-specific assortments, user groups and reporting. The exact experience depends on your platform's permissions and store capabilities.

Which platforms can you support?

We can work with supported company-store and apparel eCommerce platforms, including InkSoft, DecoNetwork, OrderMyGear, Chipply and Shopify, subject to the features available in your account.
