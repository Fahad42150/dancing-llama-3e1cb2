Frequently asked questions

What does an online store manager do?

An online store manager may oversee product information, storefront maintenance, order administration, inventory records and daily operational workflows. FJX Studio provides remote administrative support within an agreed scope.

Can you take full control of my online store?

We work with staff-level access and agreed permissions. Ownership, payment controls, legal decisions, pricing approvals and other sensitive decisions remain with your authorized team.

Can you manage Shopify or WooCommerce stores?

We can discuss product catalog, store maintenance and administrative tasks for Shopify and WooCommerce. InkSoft remains our primary specialty.

Do you support businesses in Canada and the USA?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote services to businesses serving the United States and Canada.
