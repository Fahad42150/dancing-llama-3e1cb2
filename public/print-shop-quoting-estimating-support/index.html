Specialized support for your operation

Accurate quotes start with complete job information. Garment style, colors, sizes, quantities, decoration method, print locations, artwork, setup requirements and due dates all affect the estimate. We help collect and organize those details so your estimator or shop owner can price the work with fewer missing pieces.

Customer inquiry intake

Organize customer, contact, event, delivery deadline, budget and project notes from web forms, email or your approved CRM.

Apparel & decoration specifications

Collect garment brands and styles, colors, size breakdowns, quantities, print locations and requested decoration methods such as screen printing, embroidery, DTF or DTG.

Artwork & production details

Request artwork files, record placement instructions and note whether vector cleanup, digitizing or a customer proof is still required.

Estimate preparation support

Enter approved garment costs, decoration details, setup charges, quantity breaks and shipping inputs into your quoting template or shop system. Your team sets and approves final pricing.

Quote follow-up & revisions

Maintain an open-quote tracker, record requested changes and follow up according to your schedule until the customer accepts, declines or pauses the project.

Production handoff

Prepare a clean job summary with approved quote, garment breakdown, artwork status, due date and customer notes for your production team.

Frequently asked questions

Do you independently set print-shop prices?

No. We can enter data and apply your documented rate cards or formulas, but your shop approves final pricing, margins, discounts and delivery commitments.

Can you work inside Printavo or another shop system?

We can assist with supported workflows in Printavo, Teesom, spreadsheets or another system you use, subject to account access and available features.

Can you call customers and follow up on quotes?

Phone and email follow-up can be included when the communication channels, scripts, working hours and escalation rules are agreed in advance.
