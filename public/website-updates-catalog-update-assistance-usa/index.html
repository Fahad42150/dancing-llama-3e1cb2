How our service works

Website update assistance for US businesses

An online store needs regular attention: product information changes, images are replaced, prices and availability are reviewed, seasonal pages are refreshed and outdated content is corrected. FJX Studio helps US businesses handle approved website and catalog updates through a clear task list and agreed review process.

Product catalog update and maintenance services

Support can include adding new products, editing existing product titles and descriptions, updating supplied images, entering SKUs, organizing categories, maintaining size and color variants, correcting attributes and checking for missing product information. We work from your approved spreadsheets, supplier data and instructions.

Website content and page updates

We can assist with routine edits to existing pages, service descriptions, promotional content, FAQs, banners, image placement and other supplied website copy. Larger redesigns, custom development, advanced SEO strategy and technical troubleshooting should be scoped separately so the required skills and access are clear.

Platforms and website systems

FJX Studio's primary specialty is InkSoft apparel store support, with project-based catalog and content assistance for Shopify, WooCommerce and other systems where the required workflow and permissions are available. Before starting, we confirm the platform, theme or editor, import tools and approval requirements.

A reliable website update workflow

We begin with a change list and source materials. Each task is categorized as product data, content, image, layout or technical work. Updates are completed in the agreed environment, checked against your instructions and reported for review. Your team retains final approval for pricing, policy changes, legal statements and publishing decisions.

How FJX Studio supports US website owners

FJX Studio can help with recurring catalog maintenance, product data entry, website content updates, image uploads, category organization and routine eCommerce administration. We aim to make updates consistent and traceable, not to make unapproved changes or promise a particular traffic or sales result.

Website catalog update keywords and search visibility

A well-maintained catalog helps customers understand what is available and helps search engines interpret page content. Product names, descriptive copy, structured categories, useful images and accurate attributes should be written for people first. Avoid duplicating pages or forcing unrelated keywords into product descriptions.
