Specialized support for your operation

Promotional orders involve more than entering a line item. Product specifications, decoration, quantity breaks, setup charges, proofs, supplier confirmations and in-hands dates all need to stay aligned. We help coordinate the administrative workflow so your sales and production teams can focus on customers and fulfillment.

Product research & supplier checks

Research suitable apparel, drinkware, bags, headwear and branded merchandise. Gather available colors, sizes, minimums, pricing, decoration options and estimated lead times from supplier sources you provide.

Quote & estimate preparation

Organize item costs, quantity breaks, setup fees, decoration charges and shipping inputs into your approved quote format. Final pricing and margin rules remain under your direction.

Artwork proofs & approvals

Track artwork files, digital proofs, revision notes and customer approval status. Keep the approved version connected to the correct order details.

Purchase orders & order entry

Prepare purchase-order information, enter approved order details into your workflow and compare supplier acknowledgements against the customer-approved specifications.

Shipment & delivery follow-up

Monitor order milestones, request tracking updates and flag backorders, substitutions or schedule risks for your team to resolve.

Records & status reporting

Maintain clean order logs, quote follow-up lists, supplier notes and spreadsheet reports so the current status is visible to your team.

Frequently asked questions

Can you contact suppliers for stock and pricing?

We can support supplier research and follow-up using the contacts, portals and communication process you authorize. Supplier terms and final purchasing decisions stay with your business.

Do you calculate final selling prices?

We can organize quote inputs and apply formulas or markup rules you provide. Your team approves final prices, margins, discounts and customer commitments.

Can you manage orders across different platforms?

Yes. We can work within your approved CRM, order-management platform, supplier portals and spreadsheets. Access and workflow requirements are reviewed before work begins.
