Frequently asked questions

Can you update inventory in my online store?

Yes. With authorized access, we can update approved stock quantities and availability fields from your inventory source.

Do you perform physical stock counts?

No. Physical counts and verification of stock on hand must be completed by your warehouse or staff. We can enter and organize the results.

Can you maintain inventory spreadsheets?

Yes. We can update agreed Excel or CSV-based inventory trackers and report missing or inconsistent data.

Do you offer inventory support for US eCommerce stores?
