Virtual assistant services built around your task list

A freelance virtual assistant can take ownership of clearly defined, repeatable tasks without adding an in-house role. We begin with your workflow and priorities, then agree on deliverables, access levels, review steps and communication.

Ecommerce and product catalog assistance

Delegate product uploads, listing edits, titles and descriptions, product images, SKUs, variants, sizes, colors, tags, categories, approved pricing changes and catalog quality checks. We can also organize spreadsheets and prepare product data for upload.

Online store administration

Website and administrative support

Platforms and specialist experience

Frequently asked questions

What does a freelance virtual assistant do?

A freelance virtual assistant handles agreed remote tasks such as administration, product data entry, ecommerce store updates, website content uploads and operational support.

Can I hire a virtual assistant for only a few tasks?

Yes. We can review a one-time task batch or a recurring list of responsibilities and propose a scope that fits the work.

Do you specialize in ecommerce?

Yes. Ecommerce operations, product catalog management and online store administration are core service areas, with InkSoft as a primary specialty.

How do I get started?

Send your platform, task list, estimated volume and deadline to sales@fjxstudio.com. We will review the requirements and discuss next steps.
