Ecommerce operations support

Online retailers need accurate product records, consistent listings and organized daily workflows. Our ecommerce virtual assistant service is designed for defined operational tasks that can be delegated and checked against your procedures.

Product listings and catalog management

Get help preparing and updating product titles, descriptions, images, SKUs, variants, categories, tags and other product attributes. We can also assist with catalog cleanup, spreadsheet preparation and listing quality checks.

Order and inventory administration

Support can include order detail checks, status tracking, inventory record updates, exception logs and routine queue administration. Your team retains control of fulfillment decisions, payment approvals, refunds and customer policy exceptions.

Customer service workflow assistance

We can help organize incoming customer inquiries, categorize support requests, prepare responses from your approved templates and flag cases that require your decision. Customer-facing access and response rules are agreed before work starts.

Platforms and store types

FJX Studio's primary specialty is InkSoft for apparel decorators, screen printers, team dealers and promotional product businesses. We also discuss scoped Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress ecommerce tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What does an ecommerce virtual assistant handle?

Typical tasks include product uploads, listing updates, catalog organization, order administration, inventory records and customer support workflow assistance.

Do you work with US ecommerce businesses?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote ecommerce operations support to businesses serving the United States.

Can you manage my entire online store?

We first review the platform, task volume, access requirements and responsibilities. We then agree on a realistic scope rather than assuming full account or business management.

Which platforms do you support?

InkSoft is our primary specialty. We also consider scoped Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress ecommerce support.
