Our 10-Point Pre-Launch QA Checklist

Before we send any store or catalog update back to your team, we audit the details that matter to your storefront and production workflow.

  1. Artwork & Mockup Integrity: Verify transparent PNG/SVG files (no unintended white bounding boxes) and confirm imprint boundaries are scaled to the supplied specifications.
  2. Decoration Placement Rules: Check left-chest, full-front, full-back and sleeve locations against your shop’s production specifications.
  3. Pricing & Margin Accuracy: Verify screen-print color-count tiers, embroidery stitch brackets and approved wholesale-to-retail markups.
  4. Extended Size Upcharges: Check 2XL–5XL size upcharges consistently across applicable garment variants.
  5. Supplier Swatch & Hex Matching: Compare available mill colors and hex values with the supplied SanMar, S&S Activewear and AlphaBroder references; flag discontinued or unavailable colors for approval.
  6. Personalization & Roster Logic: Test player name/number fields and configured upcharge triggers in the order flow.
  7. Store Run Time & Countdown: Verify open/close dates, timezone settings and closing messages against the brief.
  8. Fulfillment & Shipping Rules: Test the configured UPS/USPS rates, local shop pickup and school/coach bulk-delivery options.
  9. Printavo / Order Manager Handoff: Check line-item SKUs and imprint notes for the agreed Printavo or order-manager workflow.
  10. Mobile & Desktop Checkout Test: Review key pages and run an end-to-end test using the available test process to catch broken links or checkout issues.

Checks are performed against the access, test environment and specifications provided by the client. Third-party integrations and live payment processing remain subject to the relevant provider.
