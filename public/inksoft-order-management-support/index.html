What this service includes

Support is scoped around your InkSoft account, product data, permissions and operating procedures. Your team supplies and approves business-critical pricing, artwork, inventory and production decisions.

Order detail checks

Review assigned order records for required customer details, selected products, sizes, colors, quantities and personalization information. Flag incomplete or conflicting details for your team to resolve.

Proof and approval tracking

Maintain administrative records of proof requests, customer approvals and outstanding actions when those stages are part of your workflow. We follow your status definitions and do not approve artwork or production on your behalf.

Production notes and status updates

Enter approved notes, update assigned status fields and maintain task trackers so your team can see which orders need attention. Production scheduling and priority decisions remain with your business.

Customer and exception coordination

Prepare routine order updates from templates or instructions you approve, and escalate exceptions such as missing information, requested changes or delivery concerns to the designated contact.

A clear, reviewable workflow

1. Share your requirements

Tell us about your store, the products involved, the current process and the result you want to achieve.

2. Provide source data and access

Supply the approved files, account permissions, reference examples and decision rules needed for the assigned tasks.

3. Review a sample

We complete an agreed sample or first batch so your team can confirm field mapping, presentation and accuracy.

4. Complete and report

We carry out the approved work, record exceptions and hand back items that require your decision.

Frequently asked questions

Can you manage InkSoft orders after customers check out?

We can assist with assigned administrative tasks such as order detail checks, status updates, notes and exception tracking, based on the permissions and workflow you provide.

Do you approve proofs or release orders to production?

No. Your authorized staff retain responsibility for artwork approval, production release, pricing exceptions and other business decisions. We can track approvals and update records after you confirm them.

Can you help with order backlogs?

Yes. We can review a defined queue, organize outstanding actions and work through approved administrative updates in priority order.

Do you handle refunds or payment information?

Financial decisions, payment credentials and refunds remain with your business and payment provider. Our support is limited to the administrative tasks agreed for the project.
