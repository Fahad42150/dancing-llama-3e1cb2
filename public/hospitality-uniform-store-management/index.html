Frequently asked questions

Can you support restaurant uniform stores?

Yes. We can support the online catalogs and administrative workflows operated by uniform suppliers, decorators and promotional product businesses serving restaurants.

Can you organize products for different hotel departments?

We can organize supplied products into department-specific collections and store categories, based on the structure and requirements provided by your team.

Do you handle embroidery production?

No. Our role is remote store and catalog administration, including entering approved artwork and personalization details. Physical embroidery and production remain with your business or production partner.
