Our 10-Point InkSoft Product Upload QA Checklist

Every assigned product batch is reviewed against the available brief and your store standards before handoff.

  1. Product Name & Description: Check titles, descriptions, spelling and required product details against the supplied source.
  2. Supplier Style & SKU Accuracy: Match garment style numbers, SKUs and supplier references to the product list.
  3. Sizes, Colors & Variants: Review available size and color options and flag missing or discontinued variants for approval.
  4. Product Images & Mockups: Confirm the correct images and approved artwork are assigned to each relevant product.
  5. Decoration Placement: Check the requested print or embroidery locations against the supplied decoration instructions.
  6. Pricing & Quantity Breaks: Compare base prices, retail prices, quantity tiers and setup fees with the approved pricing sheet.
  7. Extended Size Upcharges: Verify applicable 2XL–5XL upcharges where those values are specified by your shop.
  8. Categories & Store Placement: Confirm products appear in the correct categories, shelves and target storefront.
  9. Personalization & Product Options: Review configured names, numbers, dropdowns and other customer options where applicable.
  10. Final Product Review: Check product pages on desktop and mobile and report missing data or items requiring your approval.

QA checks are limited to the supplied specifications, permissions and available test environment. Final pricing, product availability and production approval remain with your business.
