Frequently asked questions

What is included in team uniform store builder support?

Support can include storefront preparation, product categories, uniform listings, sizes and colors, supplied artwork, catalog uploads and launch checks.

Can you build a store for multiple teams or age groups?

We can organize agreed categories and product groupings for multiple teams, divisions or age groups based on the information and platform access provided.

Can you update uniform products after launch?

Yes. Ongoing tasks can include approved product, image, design, description, size and color updates.

Which businesses can use this service?

The service is designed for team dealers, apparel decorators, screen printers, clubs and organizations that sell or distribute team uniforms.
