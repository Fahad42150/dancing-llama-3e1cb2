Frequently asked questions

Can you manage stores for construction companies?

We support the suppliers, decorators and distributors that operate online uniform and workwear stores for construction and trade customers.

Do you certify or recommend safety equipment?

No. FJX Studio provides store administration only. Product safety ratings, compliance, suitability and purchasing decisions must be confirmed by the manufacturer, supplier and customer.

Can you update employee uniform catalogs regularly?

Yes. We can assist with recurring product, price, image, size and availability updates using the approved information and workflow your team provides.
