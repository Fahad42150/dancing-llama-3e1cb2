Frequently asked questions

Can I hire an eCommerce manager's assistant remotely?

Yes. FJX Studio can take on defined support tasks for your eCommerce manager, including catalog upkeep, order administration, task follow-ups and routine reporting.

Can you work as an eCommerce project coordinator?

We can help track assigned project tasks, deadlines, product information, handoffs and progress updates for store and catalog projects.

Yes. Our remote service is available to businesses serving the US market. Time-zone overlap and response expectations are discussed before starting.
