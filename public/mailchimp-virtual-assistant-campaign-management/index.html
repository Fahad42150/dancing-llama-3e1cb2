Frequently asked questions

Can you write email campaigns?

We can format and prepare supplied or approved content. Copywriting and strategy can be scoped separately; your team approves claims, offers and final messaging.

Will you send emails without approval?

No. Campaign content, audience and send timing require your approval before sending or scheduling.

Can you clean our Mailchimp audience?

We can identify formatting issues, duplicates and incomplete records and apply agreed changes. Consent and suppression decisions must follow your instructions and applicable rules.

Are you affiliated with Mailchimp?

No. FJX Studio is an independent service provider and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mailchimp.
