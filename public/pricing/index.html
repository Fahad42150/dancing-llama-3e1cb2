What affects your final quote?

Task complexity

Routine data entry differs from multi-step catalog cleanup, platform configuration or exception handling.

Workload and volume

Product count, order volume, number of stores and the frequency of updates help determine the time required.

Platform and access

The tools involved, permissions, training or onboarding needs and existing process documentation can affect the estimate.

Schedule and turnaround

Weekly hours, business-hour overlap, deadlines and reporting requirements are agreed as part of the scope.
