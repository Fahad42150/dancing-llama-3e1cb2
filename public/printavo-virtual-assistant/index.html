Frequently asked questions

What does a Printavo virtual assistant do?

A Printavo VA can help with assigned job and order record updates, SKU information, production notes, status tracking, file organization and administrative coordination.

Can you make production or pricing decisions?

No. Production scheduling, quote approval, pricing, purchasing commitments and customer promises remain with your authorized shop staff.

Do you support screen printing and embroidery businesses?

Yes. FJX Studio focuses on apparel decorators, screen printers, embroidery businesses, team dealers and promotional product operations.

Can you coordinate Printavo with InkSoft store work?

We can support agreed administrative handoffs and record organization between your store and shop workflows. The exact integration or data-transfer method must be confirmed for your setup.

Do you serve US and Canadian print shops?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote support to businesses serving the United States and Canada. Work hours and turnaround expectations are agreed in advance.
