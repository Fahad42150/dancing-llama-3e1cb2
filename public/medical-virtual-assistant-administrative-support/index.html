What this service includes

Support is delivered using your workflows, software access, instructions and approval rules. We agree task scope, communication channels, reporting and access permissions before work begins.

Appointment coordination

Assist with scheduling workflows, confirmations, reminders and calendar updates according to practice instructions.

Non-clinical phone and inbox support

Route routine inquiries, organize messages and escalate clinical questions to authorized practice staff.

Administrative records organization

Maintain administrative fields and organize documents in accordance with your procedures and access permissions.

Patient intake administration

Help process non-clinical intake forms and check for missing administrative information for staff review.

Referral and document tracking

Track incoming and outgoing administrative paperwork and flag items requiring staff follow-up.

Office task and report support

Maintain task lists, update non-clinical trackers and prepare routine administrative summaries.

Scope and responsibilities

FJX Studio does not provide clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment, medical coding or regulated healthcare services. Before sharing protected health information, the practice must verify applicable privacy, security, contractual and training requirements; do not assume HIPAA compliance or authorization.

Frequently asked questions

Can you use our existing software?

We can work with supported tools when access, instructions and required permissions are provided. Please identify your systems during the project discussion.

Can we start with part-time support?

The workload, schedule and coverage can be scoped around your recurring needs and agreed availability.

How are sensitive tasks handled?

We follow documented access and approval procedures. Regulated advice, licensed services and consequential approvals remain with authorized client professionals.

Do you support US businesses?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote support for clients in the United States and Canada. Coverage hours are agreed at kickoff.

How do we get started?
