Frequently asked questions

What does eCommerce bookkeeping support include?

Administrative support can include transaction data entry, expense and invoice organization, payout tracking, inventory records and preparing documents for accountant review.

Can you reconcile Shopify or payment processor payouts?

We can help organize and compare supplied order and payout records and flag apparent differences. Final reconciliation and accounting treatment should be reviewed by your accountant.

Do you prepare tax returns or provide tax advice?

No. FJX Studio does not provide tax advice, tax filing, audit services or licensed accounting opinions. Those services should be handled by a qualified professional in your jurisdiction.

Which businesses can use this service?

Online retailers and apparel businesses in the USA and Canada that need remote administrative help maintaining bookkeeping source records.
