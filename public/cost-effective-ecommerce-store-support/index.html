Frequently asked questions

What is included in eCommerce store business support?

Support may include product listing updates, order processing administration, inventory data entry, customer service assistance, website content updates and general online store administration.

Is remote eCommerce support suitable for a small business?

Yes. Small businesses can delegate specific repeatable tasks rather than hiring for a broad full-time role, provided the task scope and access requirements are clear.

How much does eCommerce virtual assistant support cost?

Pricing depends on the number of hours, task complexity, platform, order or product volume and required experience. Contact FJX Studio with your task list for a scope-based discussion.

Can I hire support for only a few hours per week?

A limited recurring workload may be possible depending on availability and the tasks required. Share your expected weekly hours and priorities when enquiring.

Which platforms can you support?

We can discuss general store administration for platforms such as Shopify, WooCommerce/WordPress, BigCommerce and other systems based on the specific tasks, access and experience required.
