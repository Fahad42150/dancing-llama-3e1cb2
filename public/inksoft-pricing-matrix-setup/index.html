InkSoft Pricing Matrix Setup for Custom Apparel Stores

Keep product pricing consistent across garments, sizes, colors and decoration options. FJX Studio helps screen printers, embroidery shops, apparel decorators and team dealers organize InkSoft pricing matrices using your approved costs, markups and pricing rules.

Discuss Your InkSoft Project

What this service includes

Support is scoped around your InkSoft account, product data, permissions and operating procedures. Your team supplies and approves business-critical pricing, artwork, inventory and production decisions.

Organize garment and blank costs

Structure the base garment costs and product data you provide, including style numbers, supplier references, colors and size ranges. We can prepare a consistent pricing worksheet before entering approved values into the relevant InkSoft product or pricing fields.

Configure size and option adjustments

Set up approved size upcharges, extended-size adjustments, color or style variations, and other product options where supported by your store configuration. Each adjustment follows your supplied pricing policy.

Apply decoration and quantity rules

Organize screen-printing, embroidery or other decoration price inputs, including agreed quantity breaks and setup charges. We do not invent production costs or decide your margins; your business supplies and approves the pricing logic.

Review pricing consistency

Check selected products and variants for missing values, inconsistent price entries and obvious mismatches against your pricing sheet. Flag exceptions for your approval before the store is published or updated.

A clear, reviewable workflow

1. Share your requirements

Tell us about your store, the products involved, the current process and the result you want to achieve.

2. Provide source data and access

Supply the approved files, account permissions, reference examples and decision rules needed for the assigned tasks.

3. Review a sample

We complete an agreed sample or first batch so your team can confirm field mapping, presentation and accuracy.

4. Complete and report

We carry out the approved work, record exceptions and hand back items that require your decision.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build an InkSoft pricing matrix from my spreadsheet?

Yes. Share your approved pricing sheet and product information. We can organize the data, map the relevant fields and enter the approved values within the access and permissions you provide.

Can you set up size upcharges and decoration pricing?

We can enter and organize your approved size adjustments, decoration prices and quantity rules where the InkSoft configuration supports them.

Will you decide my retail prices or profit margins?

No. You provide the costs, markup rules and final pricing approvals. FJX Studio handles the structured setup and consistency checks.

Can you update pricing across an existing catalog?

Yes. We can scope bulk or product-by-product updates, validate a sample first and then apply the approved changes.
