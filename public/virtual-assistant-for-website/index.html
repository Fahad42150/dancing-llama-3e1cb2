Website administration without the daily backlog

A website needs regular content updates, accurate information and organized media. Delegate repeatable tasks to a virtual assistant and keep your attention on customers and business priorities. We work from your instructions, supplied content and approved access.

WordPress page and blog updates

We can help upload and format pages, publish blog posts, update headings, add approved internal links, insert images and check page formatting before publication. Custom theme development and complex technical fixes are scoped separately.

Website product and ecommerce updates

For ecommerce websites, assistance can include product information entry, descriptions, images, categories, attributes, SKU data and routine catalog maintenance. We can discuss WooCommerce and scoped Shopify tasks.

Basic on-page content assistance

Support may include adding approved page titles and meta descriptions, formatting heading structure, writing image alt text based on image content and checking internal links. SEO strategy, technical audits and ranking guarantees are not implied.

A clear content workflow

Provide the page brief, source text, images, target URL and publishing requirements. We agree on review and approval steps so content is not published without the permissions you specify.

Frequently asked questions

What can a website virtual assistant do?

A website VA can upload pages and blog posts, format content, update images, maintain product information and handle routine website administration.

Do you work with WordPress websites?

Yes. We can discuss WordPress content uploads, page updates, blog publishing and scoped WooCommerce product tasks.

Can you help with website SEO?

We can implement supplied or agreed on-page content updates such as titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text and internal links. SEO results are not guaranteed.

Can you make technical website changes?

Routine content administration is different from development. Custom code, theme changes, integrations and technical fixes need separate review and scoping.
