Frequently asked questions

Can you manage stores for multiple plants or locations?

Yes. We can organize location-specific or department-specific storefronts when your platform supports the required access and store structure.

Can you support safety apparel and branded PPE catalogs?

We can organize supplied product information and approved branding details. Product compliance, safety ratings and suitability must be verified by your product or safety team.

Can you manage employee uniform allowances?

We can help administer supplied allowance rules and order records. Any platform configuration or payroll integration is subject to your system's capabilities.

Do you manufacture or ship uniforms?

No. FJX Studio provides remote eCommerce and administrative support; production and fulfillment remain with your suppliers or partners.

FJX Studio provides remote eCommerce and administrative support to apparel decorators, uniform suppliers and promotional product businesses serving customers in the United States and Canada. Service scope is agreed for each project.
