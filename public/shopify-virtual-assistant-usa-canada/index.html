Frequently asked questions

What does a Shopify virtual assistant do?

A Shopify VA can help with product uploads, listing edits, collections, inventory data, order administration, storefront content updates and routine back-office tasks.

Can you manage Shopify product listings?

Yes. We can create or update product titles, descriptions, images, SKUs, variants, options, tags and other supplied product information.

Can you handle refunds or payment decisions?

Financial approvals, refunds, chargebacks and sensitive account decisions remain with your authorized team. We can flag issues and update records only within agreed permissions.

Do you support businesses in Canada and the USA?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote services to businesses serving customers in the United States and Canada. Working-hour overlap is agreed before the project begins.

How do I hire a Shopify VA?

Send your Shopify task list, approximate weekly hours, product volume and preferred schedule. We will review the scope and discuss the next steps.
