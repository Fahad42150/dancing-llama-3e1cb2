Frequently asked questions

Can you calculate quotes for screen printing and embroidery?

We can prepare quote drafts using your approved rates, quantity breaks, garment costs and decoration rules. Your team approves final pricing.

Can you communicate directly with customers?

Yes, if you provide approved response guidelines, contact permissions and escalation rules. Sensitive pricing or production decisions can be routed to your staff.

What information should a quote request include?

Ideally: garment or product, quantities by size, colors, decoration method and locations, artwork, deadline, delivery method and any personalization requirements.
