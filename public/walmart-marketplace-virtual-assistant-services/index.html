Who this support is for

FJX Studio supports Walmart Marketplace sellers, consumer product brands, wholesalers and growing eCommerce businesses. Our role is to handle agreed recurring administrative tasks, maintain accurate records and flag issues requiring the seller's decision.

FJX Studio provides operational and administrative assistance. Seller account ownership, identity verification, policy interpretation, pricing decisions, refunds, financial actions and final compliance decisions remain with the seller. We do not claim affiliation with or endorsement by Walmart.

Frequently asked questions

Can you manage product listings?

Yes. We can prepare and update listings using the product facts, images, category guidance and content approvals you provide.

Can you work in our existing seller account?

We can discuss supported tasks when you provide an appropriate user role and permissions. Do not share passwords; use the platform's authorized access controls where available.

Can you handle orders and customer messages?

We can assist with routine tracking and client-approved communications. Refunds, disputes, policy-sensitive issues and other consequential actions remain with authorized seller personnel.

Do you support sellers in the USA and Canada?

Yes. FJX Studio provides remote support to businesses in both markets.

Are you officially affiliated with the marketplace?

No. FJX Studio is an independent service provider and does not claim marketplace affiliation or endorsement.
