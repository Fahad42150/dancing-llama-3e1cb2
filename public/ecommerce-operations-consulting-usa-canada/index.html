Frequently asked questions

What does an eCommerce operations consultant help with?

An operations consultant can review store administration workflows, catalog processes, order handling, task ownership, SOPs and opportunities to delegate recurring work.

Do you provide business strategy or paid advertising consulting?

This service focuses on eCommerce operations and back-office workflows, not financial, legal, paid advertising or broad corporate strategy consulting.

Can you help me hire or organize an eCommerce virtual assistant?

Yes. We can help define the task scope, SOPs, quality checks and reporting workflow, and discuss operational VA support where the work is a fit.

Which platforms do you specialize in?

InkSoft is our primary specialty for apparel and decorator businesses. Shopify and WooCommerce operational workflows can also be discussed.

Do you work with businesses in the USA and Canada?

Yes. We provide remote operations support to businesses serving both markets. Project scope and meeting availability are agreed in advance.
