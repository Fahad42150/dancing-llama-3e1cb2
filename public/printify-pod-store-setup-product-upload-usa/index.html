How our service works

Printify store setup assistance for US sellers

Starting a print-on-demand business involves more than connecting a supplier. Product choices, design files, mockups, descriptions, variants, retail pricing and store categories all need to be organized. FJX Studio provides remote operational assistance to US entrepreneurs who want help preparing their Printify catalog and connected storefront. We are an independent service provider and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Printify.

Printify product upload and listing services

We can assist with adding products from your approved product and design files, organizing product titles and descriptions, selecting supplied mockups, entering size and color variants, arranging product collections and checking listing details before publication. The exact workflow depends on your connected sales channel and the permissions available in your Printify account.

Print-on-demand catalog setup workflow

First, we review the store platform, product types, source artwork, naming rules and target audience. Next, we prepare the product records, mockups, descriptions, variant information and approved pricing data. Finally, we check the completed listings against your source instructions and provide a list of missing assets or decisions that need your approval. You retain control of final pricing, product claims and publishing decisions.

Product details that need careful checking

Print-on-demand products can have multiple colors, sizes, print areas, fulfillment options and shipping settings. We can organize supplied product information and flag inconsistencies, but production availability, provider pricing, shipping estimates and product specifications should always be confirmed in the supplier dashboard before a listing is published.

Who this Printify support is for

This service is suitable for US-based creators, online entrepreneurs, small brands, merch sellers and businesses that need help preparing a Printify catalog. It can also support existing sellers who have accumulated product updates, listing corrections or seasonal upload work.
