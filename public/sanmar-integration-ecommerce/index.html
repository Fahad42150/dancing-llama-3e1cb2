Frequently asked questions

Does FJX Studio provide a direct SanMar API integration?

Integration options depend on the current SanMar data access available to your account and the eCommerce platform. FJX Studio can assess the available feed, API or file-based workflow and support implementation or catalog operations within that scope.

Can you upload SanMar products to my online store?

Yes. We can prepare and upload supplier product information, images and variants using the data and access you provide.

Can inventory and pricing update automatically?

Automatic updates require a supported supplier feed or API, platform compatibility and appropriate account permissions. We confirm feasibility before promising automation.

Which eCommerce platforms can you work with?

Share your platform and the SanMar data method available to your account. We will review the workflow and confirm the tasks we can support.
