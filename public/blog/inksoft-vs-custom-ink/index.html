InkSoft vs Custom Ink: Store Software or Custom Merchandise Retailer?

InkSoft vs Custom Ink compares two different kinds of solutions. InkSoft is software used by decorated-merchandise businesses to run stores; Custom Ink is a consumer-facing custom merchandise provider.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · inksoft vs custom ink

Explore InkSoft Support

Understand the business models

InkSoft is a platform a print shop or decorator uses to create and manage its own online stores. Custom Ink provides a branded buying experience where customers can create and order custom products through Custom Ink's service.

When InkSoft may fit

InkSoft may suit a print shop, apparel decorator or team dealer that wants to sell through its own branded storefronts, manage products and pricing, and connect orders to its production workflow.

When Custom Ink may fit

Custom Ink may suit a group, company or individual that wants custom merchandise without operating the underlying eCommerce and production software. Compare product options, service, pricing, shipping and support for the specific order.

Ownership and customer relationship

With software, the decorating business operates the storefront and manages the customer relationship. With a managed merchandise provider, the provider controls more of the production and ordering experience. Confirm data, branding and customer ownership terms.

How to decide

If you are a decorator building your own online sales channel, evaluate InkSoft. If you are a group buying custom merchandise and do not want to manage store or production software, request a Custom Ink quote.
