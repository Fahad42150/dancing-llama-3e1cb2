InkSoft vs Chipply: A Practical Guide to Apparel Team Stores and Product Management

Compare apparel-store workflows in InkSoft and Chipply, plan supplier catalogs, organize product data and keep team-store orders ready for production.

Why apparel decorators need a connected store workflow

Decorated-apparel businesses often coordinate customer artwork, blank garments, sizes and colours, decoration locations, pricing, order windows and production instructions. When those details live in separate spreadsheets and email threads, teams can lose time correcting product listings or clarifying orders.

An online store platform can centralize parts of that process, but the platform still needs accurate product data, approved artwork and consistent operating procedures.

Where InkSoft fits

InkSoft positions its platform for print, promotional-products and decorated-apparel businesses. Its published capabilities include online stores, design tools, supplier product integrations and tools for managing products and orders. Its help center also documents supplier product setup and production workflows.

For an InkSoft store, build a repeatable product checklist: confirm the exact blank style, supplier SKU, colour names, size range, product images, decoration method, printable area, pricing rules and any personalization fields. Check the live supplier catalog before publishing, because styles and stock can change.

Where Chipply fits

Chipply describes tools for apparel decorators, including branded online stores, artwork placement, vendor connections, inventory visibility and order workflows. Its team-store workflow covers store setup, customer ordering, payment collection and production handoff.

For team, school and employee stores, confirm who approves artwork, who controls store opening and closing dates, how personalization is captured, and how consolidated orders are handed to production. Use a test order or preview before sharing the store publicly.

Supplier catalog links: start with the source

InkSoft and Chipply describe integrations with apparel vendors. For product research, use the supplier's own catalog and verify account eligibility, regional availability and stock before importing or promising a delivery date.
