How to Connect Stripe to InkSoft: Eligibility and Setup Steps

Stripe availability in InkSoft depends on the merchant’s location and account setup. InkSoft’s current help guidance says non-US-based stores use Stripe, while US-based subscribers are directed to InkSoft’s embedded Payments. Check eligibility before starting.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: how to connect stripe to inksoft

Explore InkSoft Support

1. Confirm your region and payment eligibility

Do not assume every InkSoft account can connect Stripe. InkSoft’s current help article says users based outside the United States use Stripe, while US-based subscribers should use InkSoft’s embedded payment option. If your account’s country or payment configuration is unclear, confirm with InkSoft Support before changing settings.

2. Prepare your Stripe account details

Use a business email address and have the business, owner, identity and bank information Stripe requests available. Stripe may require verification before enabling live payments. Enter sensitive information only in Stripe’s secure onboarding flow; never send card details or banking credentials to a virtual assistant.

3. Open InkSoft payment settings

For eligible non-US accounts, InkSoft’s help guide directs users to Settings > Shopping Cart Settings > Payment Settings. Follow the Stripe connection/key workflow shown in your account, complete Stripe’s email and verification steps, and return to InkSoft after onboarding.

4. Select and enable the payment gateway

Once the Stripe account is connected and any verification is complete, select Stripe in the payment gateway dropdown and enable it as directed by the current InkSoft interface. Confirm the account shows the intended enabled status.

5. Check SSL and checkout settings

Stripe requires a secure connection. If you use a custom domain, confirm the domain has a valid SSL certificate. Then review each store’s checkout settings to determine whether payment is required, optional or disabled.

6. Test before accepting customer orders

Run a controlled checkout test using the approved test process available to your account. Verify payment status, order creation, notifications, refunds workflow and reconciliation. If a payment is declined or the gateway is unavailable, contact InkSoft/Stripe support rather than repeatedly submitting live charges.
