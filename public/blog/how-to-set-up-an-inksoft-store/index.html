How to Set Up an InkSoft Store: A Step-by-Step Launch Checklist

A well-configured InkSoft store starts with a clear purpose, a clean product catalog and checkout settings that match the way your business sells. This guide walks through the practical setup sequence and the checks to complete before launch.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: how to set up an inksoft store

Explore InkSoft Store Setup

1. Plan the store before opening the builder

Decide whether the store is a team store, fundraiser, company store, event store or general catalog. Confirm the audience, opening and closing dates, product range, decoration methods, fulfillment method, shipping/pickup options and who approves artwork. Gather the logo, brand colors, product list, size charts, store policies and contact details before configuration.

2. Create the store

From the InkSoft Admin Portal, use Create New Store or navigate to Stores and choose Create Store. The Store Builder can start a store from scratch, a template or a copy of an existing store. Follow the guided steps and save progress if you need to finish later. Menu labels can change as InkSoft updates its interface, so follow the current in-account prompts.

3. Set the store name, directory and branding

Choose a short customer-facing store name and a unique store directory/URL. Add the approved logo, colors, banner imagery and store contact information. Review the storefront on desktop and mobile. If using a custom domain, complete DNS pointing, domain validation and SSL setup before promoting the URL.

4. Add products and configure decoration

Add products from available supplier catalogs or create a product manually. Check product style, SKU, color, size, images, variants, print regions, decoration method and pricing assignments. Remove options you do not intend to sell and confirm that every displayed product is actually available through your sourcing process.

5. Configure pricing, checkout and fulfillment

Set product markups and print pricing grids before publishing. Configure required or optional payment at checkout, shipping methods, pickup options, tax settings, custom checkout fields, notifications and store policies. Payment processor eligibility varies by region; verify the current payment options available to your account.

6. Test the complete customer journey

Preview the store as a shopper. Test product selection, size/color variants, artwork personalization, cart, shipping/pickup, taxes, coupon behavior and checkout. Verify confirmation emails and order routing. Place a controlled test order if your account and payment setup allow it, then confirm the order appears in the expected admin workflow.

7. Launch and maintain the store

Before sharing the link, check spelling, prices, policies, dates and mobile layout. After launch, monitor order questions, product availability, catalog corrections and closing-date tasks. Keep a simple change log so store updates can be reviewed and repeated reliably.
