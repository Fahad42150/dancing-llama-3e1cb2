InkSoft Alternatives: How to Choose the Best Fit for Your Print Shop

The best InkSoft alternative depends on what you need to replace. Some platforms focus on decorated-apparel storefronts, some on team and group stores, and others on production management or general eCommerce.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: inksoft alternatives best inksoft alternative

Explore InkSoft Support

1. Start with the job you need the software to do

List your must-haves: online team stores, online designer, decorated product pricing, supplier catalogs, quoting, production scheduling, inventory, accounting, shipping and customer management. Rank each by importance and identify which features must be native versus connected through another system.

2. DecoNetwork

DecoNetwork is a direct comparison for decorated-apparel businesses because it combines online stores with broader print and production workflows. Review its current store limits, designer, quoting, production features, integrations and pricing against your needs. Vendor comparison pages are useful but naturally present the vendor’s own perspective.

3. Shopify

Shopify is a general eCommerce platform with a broad app ecosystem. It can be a fit when you want a flexible branded storefront and are prepared to assemble product customization, print pricing, supplier and production workflows through apps or custom development.

4. OrderMyGear and Chipply

These platforms are often evaluated for team, group and pop-up stores. Compare the store-launch workflow, payment model, fees, customer experience, reporting, product customization and fulfillment tools against your store program.

5. Printavo and GraphicsFlow

Printavo and GraphicsFlow address adjacent workflows such as shop management and artwork presentation/approval. They are not exact one-for-one substitutes for every InkSoft function. Consider whether a connected stack or a single platform is better for your business.

6. Run a realistic software test

Use the same sample store, product, decoration, quote, order and fulfillment scenario in each demo. Compare total annual cost, training effort, data migration, customer experience and support response before choosing.
