2026 Apparel and Team Store Operations in the USA & Canada

A successful team store depends on more than a good design. Product setup, artwork, sizes, colours, deadlines and fulfilment instructions must work together from launch through order close.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 2026

Contact Us – Hire Us

1. Plan every team store as a project

Before building a school, club, team or fundraiser store, confirm the organization, products, decoration method, artwork approvals, pricing, size range, store dates and fulfilment plan. Assign an approver and set internal deadlines for product entry and review.

2. Keep product options and artwork aligned

Match product images to the correct garment style and colour. Check size labels, variant availability, decoration locations and approved artwork files. Use consistent naming conventions so that product records, mockups and production instructions refer to the same item.

3. Prepare for seasonal demand

Sports seasons, school calendars and fundraising campaigns can create short launch windows. Use a reusable checklist for intake, product upload, artwork review, test orders and store close dates. Recheck every season's pricing and options rather than copying old information without review.

4. Test the mobile customer journey

Many customers open team-store links from phones. Test product selection, descriptions, deadlines and checkout on a small screen. Prepare answers for common questions about garment fit, sizing, personalization, pickup, shipping and order cut-offs.

5. Delegate repeatable store administration

With clear procedures, a virtual assistant can help enter products, maintain catalog details, organize artwork files, check store settings, track launch tasks and triage customer questions.
