InkSoft vs Printavo: How the Two Platforms Work Together

InkSoft vs Printavo is not always an either/or decision. They serve related but different purposes, and InkSoft documents an integration that connects online store orders with Printavo workflows.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: inksoft vs printavo

Explore InkSoft Integration Support

1. What InkSoft is used for

InkSoft is centered on eCommerce sales and online stores for decorated merchandise, including customer-facing product design and related sales workflows. It helps a shop sell custom apparel and merchandise through branded stores.

2. What Printavo is used for

Printavo is positioned as shop management software for print businesses, with workflows such as quotes, invoices, production scheduling and job tracking. Confirm current modules and plan features directly with Printavo.

3. How the integration works

InkSoft’s help center includes an Inktavo integration guide for InkSoft online stores and Printavo. The guide explains account connection, sharing stores and how information flows into the order-management process. It also notes plan-tier requirements; verify current tiers before purchase.

4. When both may make sense

A shop may use InkSoft to sell online and Printavo to manage production and shop operations. This can reduce manual re-entry if the integration covers the fields and steps your team needs.

5. When to compare separately

If you only need one part of the workflow, compare the cost and complexity of subscribing to both versus a single platform that covers your essential needs. Consider staff training, duplicate data, reporting and integration support.

6. Questions to ask before connecting

Confirm required subscription tiers, which order fields transfer, how updates and cancellations behave, whether existing stores can be shared, and who handles integration errors. Test with sample stores and orders before going live.
