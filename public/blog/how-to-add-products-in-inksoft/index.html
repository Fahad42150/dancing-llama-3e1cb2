How to Add Products in InkSoft: Supplier Catalog, Manual and Bulk Methods

InkSoft supports several product workflows. The best method depends on whether the item exists in an available supplier catalog, needs custom setup or is being added as part of a large catalog update.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: how to add products in inksoft

Explore InkSoft Product Catalog Management

1. Add from an integrated supplier catalog

For products available in an integrated catalog, search the supplier catalog and select the correct style. Verify the product identifier, colors, sizes, images and supplier cost before adding it to your product catalog or store.

2. Use the newer Store Admin Add Product workflow when available

InkSoft’s June 2026 video describes a workflow that lets users browse SanMar and S&S Activewear products from Store Admin, decorate a blank and add it directly to a store. Availability depends on the account and supplier access shown in the platform.

3. Create a product manually

Use manual product creation for custom blanks, products absent from the supplier catalog or items requiring a custom record. Complete product information, colors, images, pricing, print regions and advanced settings.

4. Decorate products with Rapid Product Creator

Rapid Product Creator supports creating decorated products from blanks and artwork. Check print regions, colorways, decoration placement and the final storefront appearance.

5. Use bulk upload for larger catalogs

For many products, prepare the source spreadsheet carefully and use the supported bulk upload workflow. Map the required fields, normalize colors and sizes, check images and review import errors before publishing.

6. Verify every product after adding

Check product visibility, active colors, images, sizes, SKU, supplier/manufacturer, price and decoration settings. Preview the store as a shopper and test a representative product before announcing the store.
