InkSoft Vector Art Requirements: File Types, Color and Upload Tips

Artwork quality affects what shoppers can customize and what your production team can reproduce. InkSoft’s help content recommends vector formats for vector clip art and gives specific guidance for artwork used in Rapid Product Creator.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: inksoft vector art requirements

Explore InkSoft Support

1. Choose the correct file type for the artwork

InkSoft recommends SVG or PDF for vector clip art and PNG for raster/digital clip art. Vector files can scale without the same pixelation limits as raster images, but the artwork still needs clean paths, fonts converted or supplied appropriately, and sensible layer organization.

2. Use fully vectorized artwork when colorways matter

InkSoft’s Rapid Product Creator guidance recommends high-quality artwork, 12 colors or fewer, and vectorized files. It notes that colorways require fully vector artwork. If a file contains raster elements, do not assume every colorway or editing feature will work.

3. Check color count and artwork complexity

For screen printing, confirm the art’s spot-color count matches your production capability and the store’s pricing setup. Remove unnecessary effects, hidden layers and stray points. Use a clear naming convention for art versions and colorways.

4. Prepare print-ready dimensions and placement

Confirm the design fits the product’s print region and intended decoration area. Review placement, scale, transparent backgrounds and any garment-specific restrictions. The exact maximum print size depends on the product and shop process, not only on the file format.

5. Upload and assign artwork correctly

Use the Clip Art or Store Art workflow that matches the asset’s purpose. After upload, assign the artwork to the appropriate stores and categories. Preview it in the designer and confirm it appears as intended.

6. Keep source files and approvals

Retain original editable artwork and an approved production version. Make sure you have permission to use customer logos, licensed marks and supplied graphics. Ask the customer to approve a proof before production where your workflow requires it.
