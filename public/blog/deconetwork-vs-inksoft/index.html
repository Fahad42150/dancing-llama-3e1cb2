DecoNetwork vs InkSoft: Which Platform Fits Your Decorated Apparel Shop?

DecoNetwork vs InkSoft is a common comparison for apparel decorators and print shops. Both address decorated merchandise, but the right choice depends on how much of your workflow you want in one platform and which capabilities matter most.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: deconetwork vs inksoft inksoft vs deconetwork

Explore InkSoft Support

1. Core positioning

InkSoft is strongly associated with online stores and customer-facing design for decorated merchandise. DecoNetwork positions itself as a broader all-in-one platform for decorated apparel businesses, including stores and production-oriented functions. Verify current feature availability in each vendor’s live product demo.

2. Online stores and customization

Compare store creation, customer-specific stores, online designer, product catalog sources, artwork upload, proofing and mobile checkout. Build the same sample team store in both demos to assess how easy it is for your staff and customers.

3. Quotes, orders and production

Review how each platform handles quotes, approvals, job scheduling, purchasing, production tracking and customer communication. If InkSoft is paired with Printavo or another tool for parts of the workflow, include those subscriptions and handoffs in the comparison.

4. Pricing and total cost

Do not compare only the monthly headline price. Include license or onboarding charges, number of stores, staff seats, optional modules, payment processing and any third-party tools needed to achieve feature parity.

5. Integrations and migration

Ask about supplier catalogs, accounting, shipping, data export and migration. Confirm whether integrations are native, require an additional subscription or need custom development. Request a sample export before committing to a migration.

6. Which should you choose?

Choose based on the workflow your shop needs most—not on a generic winner claim. If online stores and designer-led sales are central, test InkSoft carefully. If you want more production and shop operations consolidated, evaluate DecoNetwork’s current feature set in depth.
