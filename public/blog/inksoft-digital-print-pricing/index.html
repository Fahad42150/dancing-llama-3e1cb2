InkSoft Digital Print Pricing: How to Configure a Pricing Grid

Digital print pricing in InkSoft is configured separately from screen print pricing. A carefully structured grid helps keep prices consistent across products, colors and quantity ranges.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: inksoft digital print pricing

Explore InkSoft Support

1. Enable the correct printing capability

Open Pricing > Pricing Options and review the Printing Capabilities settings. Enable Digital Printing where required and confirm the store minimums and price-display options. The exact controls available may depend on your account configuration.

2. Open Digital Print Pricing

Go to Pricing > Print Pricing > Digital Print Pricing. Review the default grids before changing them. InkSoft documentation describes default options such as Default (Dark Color), Light, Heat Press and Sublimation; the grid names and available choices may vary by account.

3. Set quantity breaks and print charges

Create quantity breaks that reflect the production method and cost curve. Enter the relevant print price for each quantity range and process. If light/dark garment treatment or a special process changes the cost, create separate grids where appropriate rather than forcing one average price across every product.

4. Assign grids to the right products

Open the relevant product and confirm the digital print grid is selected for the correct decoration method and colorway. Review any product-level minimums or overrides that may supersede the default settings.

5. Test the designer and storefront

Use representative products and quantities to confirm the designer displays the intended price. Test different colors, print areas, quantity breaks and any multiple-side rules. Compare the displayed result with your internal price sheet and update the grid if a discrepancy appears.
