Compare the store use case

Define whether you need ongoing branded stores or short-term team/spirit wear campaigns. Consider store volume, customer audience, decoration needs and fulfillment process.

Store design and customization

Compare templates, product options, online designer capabilities, personalization, artwork approval, variants and mobile checkout. Use the same sample store in both demos.

Fees and commercial terms

Request current pricing and a sample invoice. Compare setup fees, subscription or transaction charges, payment processing, store limits, payout timing and optional features.

Product catalog and production

Check supplier catalog access, decoration types, product pricing, inventory, order exports and production handoff. Confirm the workflow supports your garments and print methods.

Choose based on fit

Select the platform that supports your store program with clear costs and manageable administration. Ask each vendor to demonstrate a full store launch and fulfillment cycle.
