InkSoft Screen Print Pricing Grid: Setup, Breaks and Cost Calculation

An InkSoft screen print pricing grid helps standardize decoration charges across quantity ranges, print colors and print locations. The key is to model your real production costs and confirm how the grid is assigned to each product.

FJX Studio Editorial Team · November 19, 2026 · Target topic: inksoft screen print pricing grid

Explore InkSoft Support

1. Confirm printing capabilities first

Before creating a screen print grid, review Pricing > Pricing Options. Confirm vector/screen printing is enabled, set the maximum print colors per design and review minimums per order. These controls affect which pricing options can be used in the designer and checkout.

2. Open Screen Print Pricing

Navigate to Pricing > Print Pricing > Screen Print Pricing. InkSoft may provide default grids such as Default and Light; check their values rather than assuming they fit your shop. Create additional grids when garment color, decoration process or shop policy requires a different model.

3. Define quantity breaks

Choose quantity break ranges that reflect your production economics. A grid may use breaks such as 12–23, 24–35 and 36–47, but these are examples only. Use the ranges that match your minimums, labor, setup and order patterns. Avoid gaps or overlapping break ranges.

4. Enter setup and per-item print charges

Setup charges generally represent a one-time charge per side/design and may reflect screen preparation or other fixed costs. Print price is generally a per-item charge for each side and color configuration. Enter a value in every required cell; use 0.00 where the intended value is zero if the interface requires a numeric entry.

5. Assign the grid to products

After saving the grid, confirm each product and relevant colorway uses the correct screen print grid. A saved grid does not automatically mean every product is priced correctly. Test a sample product with realistic quantities and print locations.

6. Validate the final price

The basic pricing concept is blank product price plus print price equals finished price, with any applicable setup charges, markups, quantity discounts or other rules added according to your configuration. Compare the customer-facing result against a manually calculated sample quote before launch.
