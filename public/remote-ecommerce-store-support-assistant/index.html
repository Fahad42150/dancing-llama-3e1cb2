Frequently asked questions

What does a remote eCommerce virtual assistant do?

A remote eCommerce assistant helps with online store administration such as product uploads, listing updates, order processing, inventory records, customer support and data entry.

Can I hire a virtual assistant for my Shopify store?

FJX Studio can discuss Shopify product, order, inventory and routine store administration tasks based on your requirements and the access needed.

Do you support WooCommerce and other platforms?

General eCommerce support can be discussed for WooCommerce/WordPress, BigCommerce and other platforms. The exact scope depends on the tools and tasks involved.

Can a remote assistant work US business hours?

Working-hour overlap and response windows should be agreed during project planning. Share your preferred US time zone and required coverage when enquiring.

What should I look for when hiring an eCommerce assistant on Upwork or Fiverr?

Look for relevant platform experience, accuracy with product and order data, clear written communication, documented processes, confidentiality practices and examples of similar work. Define the deliverables before starting.

Can you provide ongoing remote online store assistance?

Ongoing support may be arranged according to workload, task scope and availability. Contact FJX Studio to discuss recurring weekly or monthly requirements.
