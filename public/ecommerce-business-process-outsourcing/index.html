Frequently asked questions

What is eCommerce business process outsourcing?

It means assigning defined online store and back-office workflows to an external service provider. Tasks can include product data entry, order administration, inventory updates, customer support and store maintenance.

Which eCommerce tasks can I outsource?

Common tasks include product uploads, catalog maintenance, order processing, inventory record updates, customer email support, data entry and routine store administration.

Can I outsource eCommerce operations without hiring a full-time employee?

Yes. Support can be scoped as a defined project, recurring part-time workload or ongoing remote assistance, depending on task volume and availability.

Do you only work with InkSoft?

No. This service is for general eCommerce operations. Platform requirements can be discussed for systems such as Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce and other online store or marketplace tools.

How do I find an eCommerce outsourcing company for a small business?

Compare task coverage, platform experience, communication, access controls, quality checks and pricing structure. Share your current workflow with FJX Studio to discuss a practical scope.

Is outsourcing more cost effective than hiring in-house?

It can reduce the need to recruit and manage a separate employee for a defined workload, but actual cost depends on task complexity, hours, service scope and required experience. Request a scope-based quote rather than assuming a fixed saving.
