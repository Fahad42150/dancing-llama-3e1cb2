Online order administration services

Reliable order administration starts with accurate records, clear responsibilities and consistent procedures. We can support the administrative steps your team assigns across an agreed eCommerce workflow.

Order detail checks

Check assigned order records for required information, product details, quantities, options and address fields against your instructions.

Order data entry and updates

Enter or update approved order information and maintain accurate records in your store or agreed tracking system.

Order status tracking

Monitor assigned order queues and update order status fields according to your documented process.

Exception identification

Flag missing information, conflicting details, unusual requests or workflow issues for your team's review.

Inventory and stock data updates

Update approved SKU, stock quantity, product availability and inventory records from your supplied counts or inventory system. Physical stock counts and adjustment approvals remain with your team.

Order queue administration

Reporting and task logs

Permissions, approvals and process

Order support is administrative. Your business retains authority over payment processing, refunds, cancellations, customer commitments, purchasing, production, shipping and fulfillment. We follow your documented procedures and escalate decisions that require approval.

1. Map your order workflow

Identify the platform, order stages, data fields, responsibilities and escalation points.

2. Set permissions and checks

Agree what can be viewed or edited, what needs approval and how exceptions are reported.

3. Maintain the assigned queue

Complete approved administrative tasks and report outstanding issues for your team.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with daily order processing?

We can discuss recurring administrative order tasks such as order detail checks, record updates, status tracking and queue organization.

Can you process refunds or payments?

Financial actions and decisions such as refunds, payment handling and cancellations remain under your authorization. We do not make unapproved financial decisions.

Do you support InkSoft team store orders?

We can discuss assigned administrative tasks around InkSoft store workflows and team-store order records, subject to your account permissions and procedures.

Do you work with US and Canadian businesses?
